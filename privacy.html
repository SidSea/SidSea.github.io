Privacy Policy

Application: Clio  ·  Last updated: 6 October 2026

Clio is a personal, private-use application maintained by Haoyang Xie ("the owner"). It is not a public service and is used only by its owner, on the owner's own data.

1. What the application accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Clio uses Google APIs (including the Gmail API) to read, search, label and otherwise manage messages in the owner's own Google account. Access is limited to the scopes shown on the Google consent screen and can be revoked by the owner at any time from Google Account permissions.

2. What the application stores

Authentication tokens issued by Google are stored locally on the owner's own device so that the application can re-authenticate without prompting. Message content is processed locally for the owner's own use; it is not uploaded to any server operated by third parties.

3. Sharing and disclosure

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to any third party. Data is not used for advertising, profiling or training machine-learning models.

4. Limited Use

Clio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Locally cached data may be deleted at any time by the owner by removing the application's local data and revoking its access in the Google Account permissions page. No copy is retained elsewhere.
