Clio

A personal OAuth application.

Clio is a private, single-owner application maintained by Haoyang (Sid) Xie. It connects to the owner's own Google account through Google's APIs so that the owner's mail client can read, search and manage his own mailbox.

The application is not offered to the public, does not create accounts for third parties, and does not collect, store or share data belonging to anyone other than its owner.

Authentication is handled entirely by Google OAuth; the application never sees or stores your Google password.
